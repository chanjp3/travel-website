Meridian

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Meridian is a trip-planning tool. It helps you plan itineraries with real flight and hotel prices and see where credit-card points or airline miles could cover the cost. Meridian does not sell travel and never takes payment. This policy covers what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Your Google account ID, email address and nameOnly if you sign in with GoogleTo keep your saved trips under your account and to apply fair-use limits on live searches
Trips you save to the cloud (destinations, dates, airports, hotel and flight picks, and any points balances you entered)When you press "Save to cloud"So you can reopen the trip on any device with its code or your account
A daily count of your live flight searchesWhen you're signed in and run a live searchTo enforce daily search limits. Counts expire within 48 hours
Trip drafts, preferences and points balancesAs you use the siteStored only in your browser (local storage), not on our servers, unless you save to the cloud

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

Services that receive your searches

To show real prices, the details of a search (airports, dates, cabin, city or coordinates) go to these providers. Your name and email are never sent to them.

  • Flights: SerpApi (Google Flights results), Travelpayouts/Aviasales (cached fares), Duffel (airline offers)
  • Award availability: Seats.aero
  • Hotels: LiteAPI
  • Maps and places: OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Open-Meteo geocoding, OpenFreeMap map tiles
  • Sign-in: Google (only when you choose to sign in)
  • Hosting: Cloudflare runs the site, the API and cloud trip storage, and processes standard request data such as IP addresses
  • Fonts: Google Fonts

Links to Google Flights, airline sites and Seats.aero open those services directly, and their own privacy policies apply there.

How long we keep it

Cloud-saved trips and your account's trip list expire automatically after 90 days. Search counters expire within 48 hours. Sign-in sessions last 30 days on your device.

Your choices

  • Use it without an account. Planning works signed out using cached prices.
  • Delete your data. Signed in, open Trips → Account → Delete my account data. This removes your cloud-saved trips and trip list immediately and signs you out.
  • Revoke Google access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.
  • Clear local data by clearing this site's storage in your browser.

Children

Meridian is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
